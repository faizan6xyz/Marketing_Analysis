Required
    1. deployment.yaml
        Runs your app containers: image, replica count, env vars, resource limits, and health probes.

    2. service.yaml
        Gives your pods a stable internal address and load-balances across them. Without it, nothing can reliably reach your app.

    3. ingress.yaml
        Exposes the app to the internet with a domain name and HTTPS. You also need an ingress controller (like nginx-ingress) installed in the cluster. A Service of type LoadBalancer can replace this for simple cases.

    4. configmap.yaml
        Non-secret configuration (URLs, feature flags, log levels).

    5. secret.yaml
        API keys, database passwords, OAuth tokens. Don’t commit real values to git; create secrets with kubectl create secret or use something like Sealed Secrets or your cloud’s secret manager.

    Optional
        pvc.yaml (PersistentVolumeClaim): if you need storage that survives restarts (SQLite files, uploads).
        statefulset.yaml: for databases like Postgres instead of a Deployment.
        hpa.yaml: autoscaling based on CPU or memory.
        namespace.yaml: to isolate your app from other workloads.
        cronjob.yaml: for scheduled tasks.
